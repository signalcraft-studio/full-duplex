About Full Duplex

Your phone is about to connect to space. Full Duplex explains what that means.

Most coverage of satellites and phones comes from the outside looking in. Ours doesn't. Full Duplex is written by an 11-year mobile network insider who saw GPRS, EDGE, 3G, HSDPA and 4G arrive, and watched Nokia rise and fall up close. We know how networks really work, and we know hype when we see it.

Now satellites are joining the network. Starlink, AST SpaceMobile and others are connecting ordinary phones straight from orbit, and that will change coverage, roaming, emergency services and how operators make money, from rural Wiltshire to the Philippines to sub-Saharan Africa. Full Duplex explains what's really happening in plain English, for anyone who works in telecoms, invests in it or just wants to know why their phone suddenly says "SAT".

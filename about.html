About Full Duplex

Full Duplex is run by Sam, a UK telecoms professional with more than ten years at Virgin Mobile, Virgin Media and Liberty Global. Sam's work has covered IVR, SMS platforms and call routing, the plumbing that decides how millions of calls and messages actually reach people.

That background shapes the site: we explain satellite-to-phone services and changes to mobile networks from an operator's point of view. That means spectrum, roaming, messaging and customer experience, not just rocket launches.

Our approach

  • Every factual claim links to a public source.
  • UK first, with global context.
  • Plain English and no hype.

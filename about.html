About Full Duplex

Your phone is about to connect to space. Full Duplex explains what that means.

Most coverage of satellites and phones comes from the outside looking in. Ours doesn't. Full Duplex is written by a mobile network insider with over 13 years in GSM, who saw GPRS, EDGE, 3G, HSDPA and 4G arrive, and watched Nokia rise and fall up close. We know how networks really work, and we know hype when we see it.

That time was at Virgin Mobile and Virgin Media (Liberty Global), from second-line technical support to IVR and SMS channel management, call routing across the estate including Virgin Media Business, thin and thick MVNOs, and work with strategic partners. The last role was Customer Access Specialist, including remote call centre setups in Pune and Manila.

Now satellites are joining the network. Starlink, AST SpaceMobile and others are connecting ordinary phones straight from orbit, and that will change coverage, roaming, emergency services and how operators make money, from rural Wiltshire to the Philippines to sub-Saharan Africa. Full Duplex explains what's really happening in plain English, for anyone who works in telecoms, invests in it or just wants to know why their phone suddenly says "SAT".

Written by Sam Beer

Starlink vs AST SpaceMobile: the UK's two-horse race for satellite-to-phone

Published 15 October 2026

Two of the UK's big mobile groups have chosen different satellite partners.

Virgin Media O2 + Starlink

VMO2 launched O2 Satellite in February 2026 using Starlink Direct to Cell over 1800 MHz spectrum.[1][2]

VodafoneThree + AST SpaceMobile

Ofcom approved a licence variation in April 2026 allowing VodafoneThree to provide D2D services in its 900 MHz spectrum, with handset exemption regulations due to take effect on 10 July 2026.[3] VodafoneThree said customer trials would start in summer 2026 through Satellite Connect Europe, a Vodafone Group and AST SpaceMobile joint venture. The aim is a service capable of data, voice and SMS.[4] It builds on Vodafone's January 2025 video call made directly to an AST BlueBird satellite from rural Wales.[4]

Why the band matters

Lower frequencies such as 900 MHz generally travel further and get into buildings better than higher ones such as 1800 MHz, although capacity and satellite design matter too. Watch this space as the trials report back.

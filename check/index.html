Can my phone connect?

Pick your country, your network and your phone. You’ll get a plain-English answer from our tracker: what’s live, what’s only on trial, what it does, and whether your phone is on the official list.

Built by a mobile network insider with over 13 years in GSM. We never ask for your IMEI and nothing you pick leaves your browser.

How the checker works

  • Network answers come straight from the Full Duplex tracker (last checked 10 Nov 2026): 31 services in 20 countries.
  • Phone answers only use official compatibility lists from networks, Apple and Google. If we don’t have one for your network and phone, we say so and show you how to check on the phone itself.
  • Lists change often. Always confirm on your network’s own page before you pay for anything.

Phone lists used (checked 13 November 2026): Apple Support (UK) · Apple: About network operator-provided satellite features on iPhone · Apple: Use Emergency SOS via satellite on your iPhone · Google Pixel Help · Google: Get emergency help through satellite with your Pixel phone · O2’s list of phones that work with O2 Satellite · T-Mobile Support · T-Mobile’s T-Satellite eligible devices list. Orange and Telstra details come from the tracker’s own sources.

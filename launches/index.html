Phone satellites in orbit: who has how many

Satellites that can connect to ordinary phones, by operator. Every number has a source and a date. If a company doesn’t publish a figure and we can’t find a reliable independent one, we say so rather than guess.

  • Starlink Direct to Cell (Starlink Mobile)

    SpaceX

    637satellites in orbit and working

    First-generation direct-to-cell satellites in two low shells: 310 at 53° and 327 at 43°. 674 were launched; 37 have come down. Works with ordinary 4G phones through partner operators such as T-Mobile and One NZ.

    As of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Starlink statistics (DTC shells 1 and 2)

  • BlueBird

    AST SpaceMobile

    12BlueBirds unfolded and in operation

    BlueBirds 1–6 and 8–13. BlueBird 7 was lost after an April 2026 launch left it in too low an orbit. Far fewer satellites than Starlink, but each one is much larger: a Block 2 BlueBird's antenna is about 2,400 sq ft.

    Plan: About 45 BlueBirds in orbit by mid-2027, and 100+ under launch contracts for 2027–28 (company target). Source

    As of 9 November 2026 · Source: AST SpaceMobile Q3 2026 business update (SEC filing, 9 Nov 2026)

  • Globalstar (satellite features on recent iPhones)

    Globalstar

    32second-generation satellites working

    24 in the operational orbit plus 8 replacements launched in August 2026 that are still moving into position. Uses Globalstar's own satellite spectrum, so only phones built for it, such as recent iPhones, can connect. Globalstar has agreed to be bought by Amazon; the deal is expected to close in 2027.

    As of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Globalstar statistics · Globalstar Form 10-Q, filed 5 Nov 2026

  • Lynk

    Elveo Mobile (Lynk + Omnispace)

    8Lynk satellites in orbit (independent catalogue)

    Lynk merged with Omnispace in August 2026 to form Elveo Mobile. At the merger, Elveo said it had 9 satellites: 6 Lynk satellites in low orbit, plus 2 low-orbit and 1 medium-orbit satellite from Omnispace. The independent count includes small Lynk test satellites, so the two figures differ.

    Plan: 320 low-orbit satellites, deployment from Q4 2027, commercial service from early 2028 (company target). Source

    As of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Lynk statistics · Light Reading, 14 Aug 2026

  • Skylo

    Skylo

    No own satellitesuses partners' satellites

    Skylo doesn't own or launch satellites. It runs its network over partners' geostationary satellites (for example Viasat/Inmarsat), and powers satellite messaging on some Android phones, such as Google Pixel. It won't appear in the launch list.

    As of 10 November 2026 · Source: Skylo: introduction

Broadband-only constellations (not phone-capable)

For scale: these networks serve dishes and terminals, not phones directly.

  • Starlink broadband

    SpaceX

    10,553satellites working, not counting the 637 direct-to-cell satellites

    Serves Starlink dishes, not phones directly. Includes 26 new V3 satellites still raising their orbits.

    As of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Starlink statistics (11,190 working in total, minus 637 DTC)

  • Amazon Leo (formerly Project Kuiper)

    Amazon

    392satellites in orbit and working

    Broadband for dishes, not phones. Amazon is building a first-generation network of 3,232 satellites. Amazon has also agreed to buy Globalstar (see above).

    As of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Amazon Leo (Kuiper) statistics · SatNews, 3 Nov 2026

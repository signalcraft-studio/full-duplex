Who puts the satellites up, and where to see the launch plans first-hand.

  • SpaceX: Starship Flight 15 (opens in a new tab)SpaceX's own page for the flight our explainers are based on.
  • Starlink (opens in a new tab)Starlink's home page: broadband, business and mobile services.
  • Starlink Mobile (opens in a new tab)Starlink's own description of its direct-to-cell service for ordinary phones.
  • Blue Origin: New Glenn (opens in a new tab)Official page for New Glenn, Blue Origin's heavy-lift rocket.

Direct-to-cell satellite companies

The companies building satellites that talk to ordinary phones, or that sit close to that market.

  • AST SpaceMobile (opens in a new tab)The BlueBird satellite builder and operator: how its network is meant to work and who its partners are.
  • AST SpaceMobile: investor relations (opens in a new tab)Quarterly updates, filings and presentations, where launch timelines and satellite counts are first stated.
  • Elveo (formerly Lynk) (opens in a new tab)Lynk's old web address now redirects here. Elveo describes itself as a direct-to-device company formed from Lynk and Omnispace.
  • Skylo (opens in a new tab)Satellite messaging for phones and devices, supplied through partner carriers and device makers.
  • Globalstar (opens in a new tab)Operator of the satellite network behind Apple's satellite features on iPhone.
  • Iridium (opens in a new tab)Satellite operator that has announced a standards-based (3GPP NTN) direct-to-device service.
  • Amazon Leo (opens in a new tab)Amazon's official page for its Leo constellation, formerly known as Project Kuiper.
  • Eutelsat OneWeb (opens in a new tab)Eutelsat runs the OneWeb low-orbit network; the old oneweb.net address redirects here.
  • Apple Support: connect to a satellite (opens in a new tab)Apple's own guide to Emergency SOS and messaging via satellite on iPhone 14 and later.

Carriers with satellite services or partnerships

Official pages from mobile operators. Each one is a live service or a named partnership we track on our own direct-to-cell tracker.

  • T-Mobile: T-Satellite (opens in a new tab)Plans, supported phones and coverage for T-Mobile's satellite service in the US.
  • O2: O2 Satellite (opens in a new tab)The UK's first direct-to-device service: price, phones and how it works, from the operator.
  • Vodafone: Satellite Connect Europe (opens in a new tab)Vodafone's announcement of Satellite Connect Europe, the European direct-to-device venture between Vodafone and AST SpaceMobile.
  • AT&T: AST SpaceMobile handoff (opens in a new tab)AT&T's account of its first handoff between a ground network and AST's satellites (2 Nov 2026). A trial, not a commercial service.
  • Rogers: Rogers Satellite (opens in a new tab)Canada's Starlink-based satellite texting service and the apps it supports.
  • One NZ: satellite data and calling (opens in a new tab)One NZ's announcement of data, app and calling support on its satellite service.
  • Telstra: satellite to mobile apps (opens in a new tab)Telstra's explanation of the satellite apps available on its satellite-to-mobile service.
  • KDDI: au Starlink Direct (opens in a new tab)KDDI's English newsroom on au Starlink Direct, its direct-to-cell service in Japan.
  • Rakuten Mobile: AST SpaceMobile video call (opens in a new tab)Rakuten Mobile's press release on its first video call over AST SpaceMobile's satellites. A trial, not a commercial service.
  • Orange: satellite SMS (opens in a new tab)Orange's announcement of a satellite SMS service in France, and the first of its kind from a European operator.

Regulators and standards

The rules and technical standards that decide what a phone is allowed to do and who may use which spectrum.

  • Ofcom: space and satellites (opens in a new tab)The UK regulator's hub for satellite licensing, spectrum decisions and consultations.
  • Ofcom: enabling satellite direct to device services (opens in a new tab)The statement and regulations that let UK phones connect to satellite services in mobile spectrum bands.
  • Ofcom: switching off 2G and 3G (opens in a new tab)What the UK 3G and 2G switch-offs mean for customers, with operator timetables.
  • Ofcom: using your mobile abroad (opens in a new tab)The UK regulator's plain guide to roaming charges and alerts when you travel.
  • FCC: supplemental coverage from space (opens in a new tab)The March 2024 FCC order that created the US framework for satellite-to-phone service.
  • eCFR: 47 CFR 25.125, SCS applications (opens in a new tab)The current text of the FCC rule that governs applications for supplemental coverage from space.
  • ITU Radiocommunication Sector: space services (opens in a new tab)The UN body that coordinates satellite orbits and radio spectrum between countries.
  • 3GPP: non-terrestrial networks (opens in a new tab)The standards body's overview of how satellites fit into 5G and LTE, which is what lets one phone work on both.
  • GSMA: non-terrestrial networks community (opens in a new tab)The mobile industry body's group for joining up mobile and satellite networks.

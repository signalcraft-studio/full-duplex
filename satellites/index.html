Meet the satellites

The satellites that can talk to an ordinary phone, and one that can’t yet. What each looks like, how high it flies, how it reaches your phone and who it works with.

Written by a mobile network insider with over 13 years in GSMGSM Global System for Mobile communications: the 2G standard from 1991 that started it all, and the base that SMS, roaming and SIM cards were built on. Glossary, from IVR and SMS to call routing and thin and thick MVNOsMVNO Mobile virtual network operator: a brand that sells mobile service over someone else’s network. A thin MVNO mostly resells; a thick MVNO runs its own core network, SIMs and billing. Glossary.

SpaceX

Talks to ordinary phones

Starlink Direct to Cell

A Starlink satellite with an extra antenna that behaves like a mobile mast. Your phone sees it as just another network to roam on.

637satellites in orbit and working · as of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Starlink statistics (DTC shells 1 and 2)

What it looks like
A flat, slab-shaped body about 4.1 m across, with solar wings that span about 29 m tip to tip. About 960 kg at launch. Source: KeepTrack: a V2 Mini Direct to Cell satellite, 2 Aug 2026
Phone antenna
A phased-arrayPhased array A flat antenna made of many small elements whose timing is adjusted to steer the beam electronically, with no moving parts. AST’s BlueBirds unfold huge ones. Glossary antenna of about 2.7 m by 2.3 m (roughly 6 m²). SpaceX calls it a cellphone tower in space. Source: AstroSpace.it, 12 Jan 2024 (SpaceX's first direct-to-cell test) · Starlink Mobile (SpaceX), accessed 13 Nov 2026
Height
Very low: about 360 km up, in two groups of orbits tilted at 53° and 43°. Source: KeepTrack: a V2 Mini Direct to Cell satellite, 2 Aug 2026 · Jonathan McDowell, Starlink statistics
How it reaches your phone
It borrows your own network’s 4G frequencies, so an ordinary 4G phone connects as if it were roamingRoaming Using another network when yours isn’t there, usually abroad. Satellite-to-phone works much like roaming: your phone attaches to a partner network that happens to be in space. Glossary. In the UK, O2 Satellite uses 5 MHzMHz Megahertz, a million cycles a second: how radio frequencies are measured. Lower frequencies (say 700 MHz) travel further and get through better; higher ones carry more data. Glossary of O2’s 1800 MHz spectrumSpectrum The range of radio frequencies. Governments license slices of it, and who owns which slice decides who can talk to your phone, which is why satellite firms team up with mobile networks. Glossary. Each satellite can point 48 beams down and listen with 16. Source: Starlink Mobile (SpaceX), accessed 13 Nov 2026 · ISPreview, 19 Aug 2026 (Ookla beam study)
What you can do today
Texts, data for 40+ selected apps, and voice calls through apps such as WhatsApp. Ordinary (native) phone calls are a future step. Source: Starlink Mobile (SpaceX), accessed 13 Nov 2026
Who it works with
T-Mobile (US), VMO2 (UK), Rogers (Canada), One NZ, Telstra, KDDI, NTT Docomo, SoftBank, Kyivstar, Entel, Globe, Airtel Africa, Deutsche Telekom and more. Source: Starlink Mobile (SpaceX), accessed 13 Nov 2026

What’s next: A second generation (V2) is planned with around 20 times the throughput of today’s satellites. SpaceX expects them to reach orbit during 2027. Source: Starlink Mobile (SpaceX), accessed 13 Nov 2026 · ISPreview, 19 Aug 2026 (Ookla beam study)

AST SpaceMobile

Talks to ordinary phones · testing

BlueBird (Block 1 and Block 2)

Few satellites, but enormous ones. Each BlueBird unfolds a huge flat antenna, so it can hear a normal phone’s weak signal from hundreds of kilometres away.

12BlueBirds unfolded and in operation · as of 9 November 2026 · Source: AST SpaceMobile Q3 2026 business update (SEC filing, 9 Nov 2026)

What it looks like
A giant flat square panel, folded for launch and opened in orbit. The side facing Earth is covered in small antennas; the back carries solar cells. Source: TechSpot, 15 Nov 2022 (BlueWalker 3 array)
Block 1 (BlueBirds 1–5)
Array about 693 sq ft (64 m²), about 1,500 kg. Launched together in September 2024. Source: KeepTrack: AST SpaceMobile BlueBirds deep dive
Block 2 (BlueBird 6 onwards)
Array about 2,400 sq ft (223 m²), more than three times bigger, and about 6,100 kg, with AST’s own AST5000 signal-processing chip. Source: Space.com, 5 Aug 2026 · KeepTrack: AST SpaceMobile BlueBirds deep dive
Height
About 520 km up, tilted at 53°. Source: KeepTrack: AST SpaceMobile BlueBirds deep dive
How it reaches your phone
It uses partner networks’ own mobile frequencies, mostly low-band, which travels further and gets indoors more easily. Examples: 700 MHz with Rakuten in Japan, 900 MHz with VodafoneThree in the UK. Source: AST SpaceMobile, 25 Oct 2024 (BlueBirds 1-5 unfolded) · ITmedia Mobile, 9 Nov 2026 (Rakuten uses 700 MHz; in Japanese) · Full Duplex: the UK direct-to-device race
What you can do today
Built for full 4G and 5G broadband. Voice calls, video calls, texts and data have been tested on unmodified phones, but there is no commercial service yet. AST is aiming for first, patchy coverage before the end of 2026. Source: AT&T, Oct 2026 · AST SpaceMobile Q3 2026 business update (SEC filing, 9 Nov 2026)
Who it works with
AT&T, Verizon and Vodafone, plus Rakuten, Telus, stc and 2degrees among others. Source: AST SpaceMobile, 25 Oct 2024 (BlueBirds 1-5 unfolded) · Full Duplex tracker

What’s next: AST is aiming for about 45 BlueBirds in orbit by mid-2027. AST says that is when service can move beyond patchy coverage. Source: AST SpaceMobile Q3 2026 business update (SEC filing, 9 Nov 2026)

Amazon

Broadband for dishes, not phones

Amazon Leo (formerly Project Kuiper)

Amazon’s rival to Starlink broadband. Today’s Leo satellites talk to dishes and terminals, not to the phone in your pocket.

392satellites in orbit and working · as of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Amazon Leo (Kuiper) statistics

Not a phone satellite (yet)
Customers connect through Amazon’s own antennas (Leo Nano, Leo Pro and Leo Ultra), not with a mobile phone. Source: Amazon, 27 Jul 2026 (Leo direct-to-device plan)
What it looks like
TBC. Amazon hasn’t published dimensions we could check, so our drawing is a generic flat-panel satellite.
Height
Three layers of orbits, at 590 km, 610 km and 630 km. Source: Wikipedia: Amazon Leo (citing the FCC authorisation)
How it connects
High-frequency Ka-band links to customer antennas and ground stationsGround station A dish site on Earth that links satellites to the internet and to the mobile network’s core. With a bent-pipe satellite, your call or message comes down here before it goes anywhere else. Glossary. That is why a phone can’t use it directly. Source: Wikipedia: Amazon Leo (citing the FCC authorisation)
Who it works with
Telecoms partners such as Vodafone, DirecTV, Herotel and Australia’s National Broadband Network. Source: Amazon, 27 Jul 2026 (Leo direct-to-device plan)

What’s next: Amazon has filed with the FCC for a separate phone network of up to 5,105 satellites using Globalstar’s spectrum, with deployment starting in 2028. Source: Amazon, 27 Jul 2026 (Leo direct-to-device plan)

Elveo Mobile (Lynk + Omnispace)

Talks to ordinary phones · small fleet

Lynk

The small one. Lynk calls its satellites ‘cell towers in space’. They are about the size of a coffee table and focus on texts and emergency alerts.

8Lynk satellites in orbit (independent catalogue) · as of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Lynk statistics

What it looks like
A flat box about 1 m across and about 85 kg. That is tiny next to a Starlink or a BlueBird. Source: KeepTrack: Lynk satellite bus, data as of 13 Nov 2026
Height
Low Earth orbitLEO Low Earth orbit: roughly 500 to 2,000 km up. Close enough for a phone’s weak signal to reach, but each satellite crosses the sky in minutes, so you need hundreds of them. Glossary on a near-polar path (about 97°), going round roughly every 94 minutes. Exact height: TBC. Source: KeepTrack: Lynk satellite bus, data as of 13 Nov 2026
How it reaches your phone
It works with standard, unmodified phones through partner networks. The merged company, Elveo, also holds rights to 60 MHz of satellite S-band spectrum and says it supports 2G, 4G, 5G and IoT devices. Source: Lynk, 12 Mar 2024 · Light Reading, 17 Aug 2026
What you can do today
Two-way text messages and emergency alerts, with a satellite passing overhead from time to time. MTN tested a voice call in South Africa in March 2025. Source: Lynk, 12 Mar 2024 · CAJ News Africa, 28 Mar 2025 (MTN and Lynk)
Who it works with
56 contracts with mobile networks and distribution partners in more than 60 countries. Elveo is testing with ten operators. Source: Light Reading, 17 Aug 2026

What’s next: Elveo plans 320 new low-orbit satellites from late 2027, with commercial service in early 2028. Source: Light Reading, 17 Aug 2026

Globalstar (agreed to be bought by Amazon)

Only phones built for it, e.g. iPhone

Globalstar

The network behind the satellite features on iPhone 14 and later. It flies much higher than the others and uses its own satellite frequencies.

32second-generation satellites working · as of 9 November 2026 · Source: Jonathan McDowell, Globalstar statistics

What it looks like
Today’s second-generation satellites weigh about 700 kg at launch and run on solar power. Source: Gunter's Space Page: Globalstar-2
Height
About 1,414 km up, tilted at 52°. That is nearly four times as high as Starlink’s phone satellites. Source: Globalstar, 19 Oct 2026 (FCC authorises C-3) · Gunter's Space Page: Globalstar-2
How it reaches your phone
It uses Globalstar’s own satellite band, not your mobile network’s, so only phones built for it can connect. The satellites act as relays, passing your message down to a Globalstar ground station. Source: Globalstar, 9 Nov 2026 (France authorises C-3) · New Space Economy, 11 Nov 2026
What you can do today
On iPhone 14 or later: Emergency SOS, messages to friends and family, Roadside Assistance and Find My location updates. A message can take 30 seconds or more to send. Source: Apple Support: connect to a satellite with your iPhone
Who it works with
Apple. Amazon has agreed to buy Globalstar; the FCC approved the deal on 27 Oct 2026. Source: Apple Support: connect to a satellite with your iPhone · FCC, 27 Oct 2026 (Amazon Leo and Globalstar)

What’s next: Nine more MDA-built replacement satellites are due on the next Globalstar launch (in our launch list). After that comes C-3, a new 48-satellite system (plus 6 spares) at 1,414 km built for phones. Launches start in 2027. Source: MDA Space, 16 Aug 2026 · Globalstar, 9 Nov 2026 (France authorises C-3)

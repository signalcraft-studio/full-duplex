Newsletter

If you subscribe, we collect your email address so we can send you the Full Duplex newsletter, Signal. Our lawful basis is your consent. Unsubscribing

Every email includes an unsubscribe link, so you can withdraw consent at any time. You can also email fullduplexhq@gmail.com and ask us to remove you.

How signing up works

When you type your email into a sign-up box on this site and press the button, your email address, the name of the box you used (for example "home" or "nav-signal") and the page you were on are sent to a small server-side helper we run on Cloudflare Workers. The helper checks the address and passes it to beehiiv through beehiiv's API, so beehiiv can add you to Signal. It does not store or log your address. Cloudflare processes the request, including your IP address, on our behalf to run the helper; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

To keep out spam sign-ups, the box uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile is a Cloudflare script that loads only when you click or tap into an email box. It may process signals from your browser and device, such as your IP address and browser characteristics, to tell people from bots. Cloudflare says it does not use Turnstile data for ad retargeting. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum.

No beehiiv scripts, forms or cookies load on our pages. If JavaScript is turned off, or you choose the link to beehiiv's own sign-up page, you go to beehiiv's site, where beehiiv may set cookies and use similar technology on its domain, including for security and to complete the sign-up. We do not set those cookies. See beehiiv's privacy policy.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served at fullduplexhq.com. We do not use our own analytics or advertising trackers, and our own pages do not set cookies.

Your browser's local storage only remembers that you have dismissed the welcome card or subscribe bar. If you arrive from one of our links, the link's source label is kept for that visit only (in session storage) so we know which posts bring readers. This is not used to identify you and is not shared with advertisers.

GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Some links on Full Duplex and in Signal may be affiliate or partner links, as explained on our disclosure page. Those cookies are set by the third party, not by us, and only after you choose to click the link. We do not control how long they last or what else the network stores; see that network's or advertiser's own privacy notice.

The network may later tell us that a click or a sale was attributed to Full Duplex. That reporting is for paying commission. Our lawful basis for handling any such reports is legitimate interests: running and accounting for the publication.

Where ordinary phones can already connect to satellites, and who's next. Every row links to a public source. Status reflects what the source says on the date checked. 'Announced' means a publicly listed partnership with no launch confirmed in our source.

For context, the GSA counted 23 operator partnerships with launched satellite-to-cellphone services as of June 2026, out of 123 announced (GSA). This table is a curated selection, not the full list.

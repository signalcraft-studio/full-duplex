Satellite-to-phone in the United Kingdom

2 direct-to-cell partnerships tracked in the United Kingdom: 1 live, 1 trial. Last checked 10 November 2026.

Virgin Media O2 + Starlink

Live

O2 Satellite: £3/month Bolt On (pay monthly, compatible phone) or included with Ultimate plans; data-only, so messaging and app-based data/voice/video work, but no native voice calls (including emergency calls)

Source: Source · Provider · Update · Last checked 9 Nov 2026

VodafoneThree + AST SpaceMobile (via Satellite Connect Europe)

Trial

AST (9 Nov 2026) targets initial, intermittent UK coverage before end-2026, with the first step a roll-out to strategic partners; Vodafone's earlier guidance was beta testing in early 2027, once AST has about 45 satellites in orbit; targeting data, voice, SMS

Source: Source · Update · Q3 · Last checked 10 Nov 2026
